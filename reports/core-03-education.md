# CORE-03 — Investor Education & Resilience Hub Report

## Executive Summary

This report documents the expanded education hub under `/learn` designed to build long-term investor resilience against financial fraud and deceptive return promises.

---

## 1. Education Architecture

### A. 10 Investor-Resilience Verification Cards
1. **Guaranteed Returns Trap** (`/calculator`)
2. **Pay First, Withdraw Later** (`/learn/scams/part-time-job-scam`)
3. **VIP Telegram / WhatsApp Groups** (`/learn/scams/telegram-vip-signal-group`)
4. **Copy Trading Misuse** (`/learn/scams/telegram-vip-signal-group`)
5. **Crypto Staking / Mining Claims** (`/learn/lessons/risk-vs-return`)
6. **Fake Trading Applications** (`/learn/scams/fake-trading-app-fql`)
7. **Fake IPO & Allotment Claims** (`/learn/lessons/who-regulates-what`)
8. **Recovery Scams (Second-Wave Fraud)** (`/learn/scams/part-time-job-scam`)
9. **OTP & Remote Access Software** (`/learn/lessons/who-regulates-what`)
10. **Coercive Threat (Disconnection / Arrest)** (`/learn/scams/utility-bill-disconnection`)

### B. Statutory Market Ecosystem Coverage
- **SEBI**: Securities and stock market regulator.
- **RBI**: Banking, monetary policy, and deposit insurance regulator.
- **NSE / BSE**: Recognized stock exchanges.
- **NSDL / CDSL**: Authorized depositories holding digital Demat securities.
