# CORE-04 — Advanced Investor Education Implementation Report

## 1. Overview & Architectural Goals

The **CORE-04 Investor Education Expansion** upgrades SANGYAN into a plain-language financial literacy and investor-protection platform while preserving the non-negotiable product safety boundaries:

$$\text{DETERMINISTIC} \rightarrow \text{STRUCTURED SEMANTIC REASONING} \rightarrow \text{TYPED DECISION} \rightarrow \text{RAG EXPLANATION}$$

The educational taxonomy strengthens user resilience against financial scam archetypes by explaining market mechanics, statutory regulators, and verification methods rather than giving investment advice.

---

## 2. Educational Taxonomy Coverage (10 Core Domains)

SANGYAN covers 10 primary financial literacy and market instrument domains on `/learn`:

1. **Mutual Funds**: NAV calculation, unit allotment, expense ratios (0.5%–2.25% SEBI cap), market-linked volatility, lump-sum vs SIP compounding, redemption T+2 cycles, non-guaranteed historical returns.
2. **Fixed Deposits**: Principal safety, fixed interest rates, tenure compounding, maturity values, DICGC bank deposit insurance (up to ₹5 Lakh per bank per depositor), premature withdrawal penalties.
3. **Bonds & Debt Securities**: Principal face value, coupon rates, yield to maturity (YTM), credit default risk (CRISIL/ICRA AAA to D ratings), interest-rate price sensitivity.
4. **Equity & Shares**: Fractional ownership, real-time price discovery on NSE/BSE, dividends, capital appreciation, market risk, historical returns vs future volatility.
5. **IPO & Pre-IPO Allotment**: Public issue process, ASBA bank net-banking funds blocking, oversubscription lottery allotment, listing gains, pre-IPO unlisted share scam cautions.
6. **Futures & Options (F&O)**: Derivatives mechanics, futures obligating contracts, call/put options, strike prices, option premium decay (Theta), high margin leverage, SEBI 89% retail trader loss study.
7. **Commodities**: Physical goods exposure (Gold, Silver, Crude Oil, Agri), MCX exchange structure, spot price linkage, margin leverage volatility.
8. **Crypto & Digital Assets**: Decentralized virtual digital assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT), wallet custody, staking/mining claims, 30% VDA flat tax + 1% TDS (Sec 194S), zero statutory investor compensation fund.
9. **Copy Trading Accounts**: Master-follower account mirroring, leverage amplification, fake profit screenshots, unauthorized pooled management circulars by SEBI.
10. **Statutory Regulatory Ecosystem**: Plain-language roles of SEBI, RBI, NSDL, CDSL, NSE, and BSE with verified source grounding.

## 3. 20 Investor-Resilience Cards

`/learn` features 20 structured Investor-Resilience Cards:

1. **Guaranteed Returns Trap**: Explains why 30% monthly or 2% daily guaranteed yield claims are mathematically impossible in regulated markets.
2. **Pay First, Withdraw Later**: Teaches advance-fee pattern detection where fraudsters demand TDS or processing fees to release promised gains.
3. **VIP Telegram / WhatsApp Groups**: Exposes staged social proof, fake profit screenshots, and finfluencers coercing transfers into personal accounts.
4. **Copy Trading Misuse**: Warns against sharing trading account passwords or API keys with unregistered master accounts.
5. **Crypto Staking / Mining Claims**: Distinguishes blockchain smart-contract staking from fake web app daily profit schemes.
6. **Fake Trading Applications**: Explains why direct APK sideloading does not prove SEBI registration or Demat escrow custody.
7. **Fake IPO & Allotment Claims**: Teaches ASBA net-banking blocking rules to prevent sending money to individual promoter UPI IDs.
8. **Recovery Scams (Second-Wave Fraud)**: Explains how scammers contact previous victims posing as recovery cells for upfront legal fees.
9. **OTP & Remote Access Software**: Teaches risks of AnyDesk/TeamViewer installation and 6-digit OTP disclosure.
10. **Coercive Threat (Disconnection / Arrest)**: Exposes artificial time urgency and panic tactics used in fake utility bill disconnection or CBI threat calls.
11. **Withdrawal-Fee / Tax Demands**: Explains that statutory TDS is deducted at source by authorized entities and never paid to personal UPIs.
12. **Fake Investment Portals**: Teaches domain typosquatting checks (.top, .xyz lookalike domains).
13. **Impersonation of Regulators / Officers**: Clarifies that SEBI/RBI officers never conduct police interrogations on WhatsApp video calls.
14. **Account Takeover (ATO)**: Teaches identity verification over regular phone calls when friends request emergency transfers.
15. **Job / Task Investment Schemes**: Explains progressive task deposit traps where victim deposits escalate before withdrawals are blocked.
16. **Payment to Personal Account Requests**: Enforces SEBI rules requiring investment funds to move strictly through corporate escrow accounts.
17. **Phishing / Malicious Links**: Warns against clicking shortened SMS links bit.ly for bank KYC updates.
18. **Mule-Payment Requests**: Teaches money laundering laws (PMLA) prohibiting renting bank accounts to third parties.
19. **Digital Arrest Threat Schemes**: Exposes the fake NCB/CBI parcel video call scam and reinforces that Indian law has no provision for "Digital Arrest".
20. **Novel & Unknown Suspicious Financial Patterns**: Teaches users that financial offers can be high-risk compositionally even if they do not fit a familiar scam name, reinforcing open-world analysis.

## 4. Product Safety & Non-Advisory Boundaries

- Zero stock tips or buy/sell recommendations.
- Zero price forecasts or market predictions.
- Zero platform/broker promotion or affiliate links.
- Non-advisory disclaimers on every educational page.
